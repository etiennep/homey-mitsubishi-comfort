Control your Mitsubishi Electric ductless mini-splits and heat pumps that use the Mitsubishi Comfort app (formerly Kumo Cloud) and a Wi-Fi interface such as the PAC-USWHS002-WF-2. Change the mode, temperature, fan speed and vane direction from Homey, follow the room temperature, and build Flows around comfort at home. Changes made with the remote or the Comfort app show up in Homey within seconds.

This is an unofficial community app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mitsubishi Electric. It signs in to the Mitsubishi Comfort cloud with your Comfort account, the same way the Comfort app does, and keeps your login on your Homey so it can stay signed in; your credentials are only sent to Mitsubishi. Because Mitsubishi no longer provides local access keys for new installations, an internet connection is needed. Units for which you already have a local access key can be controlled directly on your home network instead.
